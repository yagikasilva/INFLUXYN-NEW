How Much Does a Website Cost in Sri Lanka in 2026?

By Asantha Yagika Silva · 2026-09-15 · 5 min read

A practical guide to website pricing in Sri Lanka: what affects the cost, typical price ranges by website type, and what to check before you hire a developer.

Website prices in Sri Lanka vary widely, from a few thousand rupees for a template page to several hundred thousand for a custom platform. Knowing what drives the cost helps you compare quotes fairly.

What changes the price

The main factors are the number of pages, whether you need custom design instead of a template, e-commerce features, integrations such as payment gateways or booking, and how much SEO work is included. Support and maintenance after launch also matter.

Typical ranges by website type

  • A 1–3 page business site: roughly Rs. 15,000 and up.
  • A 5–10 page company site with blog and analytics: roughly Rs. 25,000 and up.
  • A site with an online store and payment gateway: roughly Rs. 40,000 and up.
  • A large custom platform or marketplace: roughly Rs. 80,000 and up.

These are the starting prices for INFLUXYN packages. Your quote depends on scope, so request a custom quote for anything unusual.

What to check before you hire

  • Is hosting and a domain included, and for how long?
  • Is the site mobile-friendly and SEO-ready, with titles, descriptions and a sitemap?
  • How many months of support are included?
  • Who owns the design and code once you have paid?

Ask for a written scope. A transparent price with no hidden setup fees is easier to compare than a low headline number with paid extras.
